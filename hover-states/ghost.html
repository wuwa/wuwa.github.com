Ghost state layers

A ghost component has no surface, so the state is the layer, and direction has to come from the backdrop rather than from the component. A: a neutral ink layer via color-mix(). B: the same ink layer plus backdrop-filter: saturate() brightness(). C: always a white layer and always brightness() < 1, which composes to a + (1−a)·b·C — an affine squeeze toward a fixed point, so it darkens light backdrops and lightens dark ones with no direction logic at all. B is a superset of A: at zero gain those two columns are identical. C needs no ink and no flip, and pays for it with a dead zone at its crossover instead of a seam between two ramp steps.
